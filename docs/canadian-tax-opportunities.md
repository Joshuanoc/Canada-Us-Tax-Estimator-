# Canadian tax opportunity catalog (2026)

This is a **screening catalog**, not an eligibility decision or a tax calculator. Entries must not automatically lower calculated tax or increase refunds. Each requires tax-year-specific federal/provincial rules, taxpayer evidence, and tests before being activated in calculations.

The catalog separates deductions, non-refundable credits, refundable credits, benefits and planning programs. Categories below are navigational and are not substitutes for a full legal classification.

For each entry, use four states: `needs_information`, `possibly_eligible`, `ineligible`, `verified_eligible`. Do not show `verified_eligible` without a documented rule evaluation and required supporting information.

Important guardrails:
- Canadian spousal amount depends on spouse's net worldwide income and other criteria, even if spouse lives abroad.
- Eligible dependant amount cannot generally be stacked with a spousal claim for the same circumstances.
- CCB and GST/HST credit are separate benefit programs; the Home Buyers' Plan is not a deduction.
- A larger tax refund can reflect withholding rather than lower tax liability. Refund calculation requires income tax withheld.
- 2026 figures and province-specific eligibility require authoritative CRA/provincial verification before launch.
