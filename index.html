      Methodology at a glance

      Canada cash flow2026 CPP/CPP2 and EI are included for supported non-Quebec employment cases. Federal basic personal credit and Canada Employment Amount are applied.
      Marginal analysisTaxMetric estimates the extra income tax on the next $1,000 of ordinary employment income.
      No fake precisionUnsupported state taxes, investment-income rules, special US deductions, provincial surtaxes and complex mixed employment/self-employment cases remain visibly excluded.
      TaxMetric is an educational decision-support estimator, not tax advice. Actual liability may differ because of credits, payroll rules, surtaxes, AMT, special income rules and jurisdiction-specific provisions.
