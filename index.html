Methodology at a glance

The product is deliberately explicit about what it knows and what it does not.

Official tax-year basis2026 US federal brackets and standard deductions; 2026 Canadian federal and selected provincial/territorial brackets.
No fake precisionUS state tax, investment-income treatment and unsupported pass-through entity tax are not replaced with arbitrary flat rates.
Transparent scopeEvery result lists included and excluded assumptions so users can judge whether the estimate fits their situation.
TaxMetric is an educational decision-support estimator, not tax advice. Actual liability can differ because of credits, payroll contributions, surtaxes, alternative minimum taxes, special income rules, deductions and jurisdiction-specific provisions.
Methodology

How TaxMetric calculates

Last reviewed for the 2026 tax year. The calculator favors explicit limitations over misleading precision.

United StatesFederal ordinary-income brackets and standard deductions are modeled. Estimated self-employment tax is included. State income tax is not modeled.
CanadaFederal and selected province/territory progressive brackets are modeled before many credits, surtaxes and payroll contributions.
BusinessUS C-corp federal tax and Canadian general federal corporate tax are modeled. Pass-through entities are not assigned invented entity-level rates.
